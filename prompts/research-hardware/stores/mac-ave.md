# Mac-Ave

Apply [the shared listing contract](../listings.md). Start at <https://www.mac-ave.com/> and follow Shop Used or
search `site:mac-ave.com/product/` with the exact Mac model, chip and RAM size.

Product pages expose sale price, crossed-out old price, installed memory, storage, chip, SKU and condition. Use the
sale price tied to that complete machine. Separate M1/M2/M3/M4, Max/Ultra/Pro variants and GPU-core configurations;
RAM capacity alone does not identify their bandwidth. Obtain bandwidth from Apple's exact-model specifications.

Check the explicit stock message before accepting the price. The tested M1 Ultra 64GB page said
“Discontinued / Out Of Stock” and must be rejected despite its displayed sale price. If another listed machine is
available, classify unified-memory Macs consistently with HWDB's GPU-system convention. Return the shared result.
